Partner Regions

Three regional programs

Each regional program involves direct collaboration with national and sub-national education partners on data collection, validation, and capacity building.

🇵🇭
Philippines
In collaboration with DepEd & ANSA-EAP

School-level EMIS data from the Department of Education's BEIS system, with a focus on equity, WASH, and learning outcomes in Mindanao and the Bangsamoro region.

🇰🇭
Cambodia
In collaboration with MoEYS & regional partners

Sub-national EMIS data collection with a focus on school infrastructure, teacher deployment, and enrollment across rural and peri-urban areas.

🇳🇬
Nigeria
In collaboration with state-level MoEs

State-level school census data focusing on out-of-school children, gender parity, and resource gaps — complementing national EMIS which has significant coverage limitations.

Project Structure

Two integrated components

01
Regional EMIS Data Collection

Direct partnership with national and sub-national education ministries and civil society organizations to collect, validate, and geocode school-level administrative census data beyond what is publicly available — filling gaps in the OEDC dataset and enabling longitudinal analysis.

02
Deep Learning Indicator Estimation

Deep learning models trained on GEDEx partner data to estimate missing EMIS indicators — enrollment, resources, outcomes — in data-sparse schools and regions. Models use satellite imagery, spatial context, and auxiliary signals to generalize beyond the training regions.

Dataset Structure

Four joinable dimensions

Every country is structured as four flat CSV files sharing a common oedc_id key.

📍
Geo
{ISO3}_geo.csv

School identity, geocoordinates, administrative hierarchy, coordinate precision tier, and GHSL urban/rural classification. One row per school.

👩‍🏫
Personnel
{ISO3}_personnel.csv

Total enrollment, sex-disaggregated enrollment, teaching staff counts, qualified teacher proportions, and pupil-teacher ratio. One row per school × year.

🏗️
Resources
{ISO3}_resources.csv

WASH access (JMP-aligned), electricity, internet connectivity and type, computer lab, library, and permanent building status. One row per school × year.

📊
Outcomes
{ISO3}_outcomes.csv

Promotion, repetition, and dropout rates (UIS reconstructed cohort method), completion rate, and gross intake ratio where available. One row per school × year.

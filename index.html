About GEO

Building the data infrastructure that education research needs

GEO is a research program at the intersection of GeoAI, spatial data science, and international development — focused on making school-level data accessible, comparable, and useful across low- and middle-income countries.

All datasets are freely available for academic research and humanitarian use under open licenses. Models and code are released on GitHub.
